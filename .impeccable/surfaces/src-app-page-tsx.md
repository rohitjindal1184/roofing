# Homepage surface brief

- Scope: `src/app/page.tsx`; Persuade mode.
- Audience and job: Bay Area homeowners and property owners, often anxious about an active leak or unsure whether a roof needs repair or replacement. Help them reach a credible next action without making them decode contractor jargon.
- Primary actions: call the 24/7 line for an active leak; use the contact route for planned inspection, repair, or replacement work.
- Proof: inspect-first/quote-second process, 15+ person in-house team, 300+ completed projects, 250+ satisfied clients, written warranty, real project photography, and 31 named local service areas.
- Constraints: preserve site facts and legacy routes; no fabricated reviews, ratings, prices, or response promises; no stock additions; contact submissions are not yet delivered by the backend.
- Direction: an inspection dossier, staged decision-first. The opening separates emergency from planned work; the rest unfolds as process, scope ledger, crew proof, project evidence, and local coverage.
- Memorable moment: “Your roof is talking” beside a real crew portrait, followed immediately by the emergency/planned decision lanes.
- Unresolved: contact delivery and spam protection remain an implementation gap outside this visual redesign.
